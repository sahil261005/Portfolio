VeriFrame

Explainable video forensics that tells real footage from deepfakes and AI-generated video.

Instead of one black-box score, a panel of specialist agents (face-swap, motion, audio, C2PA provenance, vision LLM) examines each video in parallel. A LangGraph consensus engine returns AUTHENTIC, MANIPULATED or UNCERTAIN, with a reason for every flagged frame.
